Those three days ♡

Some days end.
Some feelings stay.

Pata hai Betu, agar mujhse koi pooche ki meri life ke sabse beautiful moments kaun se the, toh shayad main bina soche un teen dino ke baare mein bataunga.

Tumhara apna soft corner share karna, humara comfort, woh cuddles, bina hesitation apni baatein keh paana… tumhare saath sab kuch itna naturally apna laga.

Aur woh tumhare haathon se khilayi hui biryani! Shayad taste se zyada usmein tumhara pyaar tha. Tumhari gaane sunte waqt wali cute si harkatein, chutkiyan bajana, palm ghumakar music feel karna, cab ride ki baatein, humara hasna… sab yaad hai.

Last day tumhe itne khulkar hanste dekhkar bas yahi laga tha ki tumhari smile hamesha aisi hi rahe. Bus mein tumhara mujhe hug karke sulana — woh sukoon main kabhi nahi bhoolunga.

I don't just miss those three days, Betu. I miss how beautifully we felt together. I want us to keep making moments like those, for a lifetime.

✦ A LETTER FOR YOU ✦ONLY YOU

My Princess,

I'm truly, deeply sorry.

Betu, kal jo hua uske baare mein sochkar mujhe sach mein bahut bura lag raha hai. Sabse zyada takleef is baat ki hai ki jis insaan ki smile mere din ko better bana deti hai, meri hi wajah se uski aankhon mein aansu aa gaye.

Mujhe maaf kar dena, meri jaan. Tumne apni life mein jo kuch bhi dekha aur face kiya, woh tumhari kahaani ka hissa hai. Mujhe kabhi bhi tumhare past ko baar-baar saamne laakar tumhe pareshan nahi karna chahiye tha. Mujhe tumhe samajhna chahiye tha, tumhari baat sunni chahiye thi aur tumhe ye feel karwana chahiye tha ki tum mere saath safe ho.

Main nahi chahta ki tumhe mujhse kuch share karne se pehle sau baar sochna pade. Main nahi chahta ki tum mere saamne apni feelings chhupao ya ye socho ki kahin koi purani baat phir se tumhe hurt na kar de. Tum mere saath apni har baat bina darr aur bina hesitation keh sako — main aisa bharosa earn karna chahta hoon.

Tum kisi se comparison nahi ho, Betu. Tum meri Shreya ho. Main tumhe tumhare aaj ke liye, tumhari smile ke liye, tumhari chhoti-chhoti nautanki ke liye, tumhari kindness aur tumhare poore existence ke liye choose karta hoon. Tumhara past tumhari identity nahi hai, aur na hi use kabhi tumhare present ke pyaar par bhaari padna chahiye.

Main ye promise nahi karunga ki humari life mein kabhi arguments ya misunderstandings nahi hongi. Lekin main ye zaroor promise karta hoon ki apni galti ko justify karne ke bajaye samajhne ki koshish karunga. Pehle tumhari baat sununga, tumhari feelings ko lightly nahi lunga, aur apne words aur actions dono mein zyada care rakhunga.

Main tumhe apni wajah se kabhi rone nahi dena chahta. Aur sirf ye keh dena kaafi nahi hai — mujhe apne behaviour se tumhe ye feel karwana hoga. Tumhe mere saath tension nahi, sukoon mile. Darr nahi, bharosa mile. Aur kabhi ye doubt na ho ki tum mere liye kitni important ho.

I am not promising to be perfect. I am promising to take responsibility, to keep learning, and to become a better partner — not just in words, but in the little things I do every day.

Tumhari woh khulkar hansne wali smile mujhe bahut pyaari hai. Main chahta hoon ki hum dono milkar aisi zindagi banayein jahan hum ek doosre ko aur zyada samjhein, ek doosre ka sukoon banein, aur har din thoda aur achha pyaar karna seekhein.

I'm sorry, meri Princess. I love you — and I want to love you more thoughtfully, more patiently, and more gently. ♡
